# 釣魚大作戰 | Fishing Frenzy

一款支援繁體中文與英文切換的 3D 釣魚小遊戲，可在手機、平板和電腦上遊玩。

A 3D-style fishing game with Traditional Chinese and English. Play on phones, tablets, and computers.

## 開始遊玩 | Play

開啟 GitHub Pages 網站即可遊玩。拖動魚竿柄，等魚張開嘴時用魚鈎鈎起，再把魚放入透明桶得分。

Open the GitHub Pages site to play. Drag the rod handle, hook a fish when its mouth opens, then drop it into your clear bucket to score.

## 功能 | Features

- 單人挑戰與雙人對戰 | Solo and 2-player modes
- 1、2 或 3 圈魚盤 | Choose 1, 2, or 3 pond rings
- 慢、中、快三種速度 | Slow, medium, and fast speeds
- 音樂與音效 | Music and sound effects
- 中英文介面切換 | Traditional Chinese and English interface
- 支援觸控和滑鼠 | Touch and mouse controls
